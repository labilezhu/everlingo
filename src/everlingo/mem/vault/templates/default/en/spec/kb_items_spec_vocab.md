# 曖昧

## Explanation for me

`曖昧` means "unclear, unspecified, ambiguous".

It can describe unclear language as well as unclear attitudes, relationships, or boundaries.

## Common usage

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 曖昧な返事 | vague answer |
| 曖昧な関係 | ambiguous relationship |
| 意味が曖昧 | unclear meaning |

## Example sentences

> 彼の返事は曖昧だった。
> His answer was vague.

> この文章の意味は少し曖昧です。
> The meaning of this sentence is a little unclear.

## Points I often confuse

`曖昧` is not exactly the same as the Chinese "暧昧".

Chinese "暧昧" often leans toward relationships; Japanese `曖昧` is broader and can mean any "unclear".

## Memory hook

Think of it as:

> The boundary was not drawn clearly.
