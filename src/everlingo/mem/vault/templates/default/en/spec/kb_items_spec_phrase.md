#### markdown body content

# take for granted

## Meaning

`take something for granted` means:

1. to believe something is true/obvious;
2. to fail to recognize the value of something;
3. to assume something always holds.

## Example sentence

> We shouldn't take backward compatibility for granted.

## Common mistake

Do not literally translate it as:

> take it as granted

A more natural phrase is:

> take it for granted
