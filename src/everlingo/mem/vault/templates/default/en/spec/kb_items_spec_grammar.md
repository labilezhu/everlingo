# て形

## What it is

The `て-form` is a connective form of Japanese verbs, commonly used to link actions, requests, ongoing states, and so on.

## Common uses

| Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| request | 見てください | Please look |
| linking actions | 朝ご飯を食べて、学校へ行く | Eat breakfast, then go to school |
| ongoing state | 本を読んでいる | I am reading a book |

## My current problems

- Not yet familiar with godan (五段) verb conjugation.
- Easy to confuse the "ongoing" and "result state" uses of `ている`.
